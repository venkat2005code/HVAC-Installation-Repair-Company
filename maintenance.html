Maintenance FAQ

How often should I service my HVAC system?

We recommend at least one tune-up per season — ideally before summer for your AC and before winter for your heating system. Our Standard and Premium plans cover this automatically.

What is included in the 21-point audit?

Our technicians test capacitors, inspect contactors, measure delta-T, check subcooling and superheat values, measure blower amp draw, flush condensate drains, and 15 additional diagnostic checks — all documented in a written report.

Can I cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. Our maintenance plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel or upgrade your plan any time by calling our office or through your account portal.

Does the plan cover parts and repairs?

Tune-up labor is fully covered. Parts and repairs are discounted — 15% off on Standard, and the Premium plan waives the dispatch fee on all service calls. Repairs themselves are billed at our standard flat-rate pricing.

Diagnostics & Repair

AC Repair

Something not right with your AC? Our technicians carry 95% of common parts on every truck so most repairs are completed same day.

Not Cooling Properly

Low refrigerant, dirty coils, or a failing compressor — we pinpoint the root cause with pressure testing and electronic leak detection.

Avg. Repair: 2–3 hrs

Water Leaks / Ice Buildup

Frozen evaporator coils or a clogged condensate drain can cause water damage. We resolve both the symptom and underlying cause.

Avg. Repair: 1–2 hrs

Strange Noises

Banging, rattling, or squealing points to a failing motor, loose hardware, or failing bearings. We stop the noise and prevent a full breakdown.

Avg. Repair: 1–2 hrs

Unit Won't Turn On

Tripped breakers, failed capacitors, or faulty contactors are among the most common no-start causes — we carry all on the truck.

Avg. Repair: 1 hr

Weak Airflow

A failing blower motor, collapsed duct, or severely clogged filter restricts airflow. We restore full delivery to every register.

Avg. Repair: 2–4 hrs

High Energy Bills

An inefficient or failing system works harder and costs more. We perform a full efficiency audit and tune your system to peak performance.

Avg. Audit: 90 min

System Upgrades

HVAC Replacement & Upgrades

If your system is over 12 years old or repairs cost more than 50% of replacement value, it's time to upgrade. A modern system pays for itself in energy savings within 5–7 years.

Signs It's Time to Replace:

  • System is 12+ years old
  • Repair cost exceeds 50% of unit value
  • Energy bills increasing year over year
  • Uses outdated R-22 refrigerant (now banned)
  • Inconsistent temps — hot/cold zones throughout home

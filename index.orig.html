Who We Are

A Legacy Built on Comfort

Founded in 1999 by master technician Robert Airdale, AirPro HVAC began as a two-person operation with one simple mission: provide honest, expert HVAC service that homeowners could actually trust.

Today, we operate a team of 45+ certified technicians serving the greater metropolitan area, completing over 8,000 service calls each year. Yet our core values remain unchanged — integrity, excellence, and genuine care for every customer.

We're proud to be the #1 rated HVAC company in our region for the 5th consecutive year, recognized for our 98% customer satisfaction rate and same-day service guarantee.

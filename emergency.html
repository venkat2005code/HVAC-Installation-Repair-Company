Common Questions

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

Only the $149 dispatch fee applies for after-hours calls. All labor and parts are billed at our standard daytime rates. No weekend surcharges, no holiday markups.

What if you can't fix it in one visit?

Our trucks carry 95% of common parts. If a specialty part is needed, we'll stabilize your system and return within 24 hours — at no additional dispatch fee.

Is the 90-minute guarantee binding?

Yes. If we arrive after 90 minutes from the time of your confirmed call, the $149 dispatch fee is fully waived. No questions asked.

Do technicians carry ID and certification proof?

Every technician carries a photo ID, their EPA 608 certification, and a background-check clearance badge. You'll also receive a text with their name and photo before they arrive.
